2. D&D 5e details

Open a list to read each option, or press 🎲 for a random pick.

New to D&D? Start here

When your character tries something risky, you roll a 20-sided die (a "d20") and add a bonus from their sheet. If the total reaches the target number, it works.

Most of the numbers here are those bonuses and targets, so higher is better. Tap any ⓘ to see what a term means.

2. Call of Cthulhu details

Investigators are ordinary people in the 1920s, using 7th Edition rules.

New to Call of Cthulhu? Start here

When your investigator tries something risky, you roll percentile dice for a number from 1 to 100. If you roll at or under their score, it works. So a skill of 60 means a 60% chance.

Investigators are ordinary people, not heroes. Fighting rarely ends well, so clever thinking and running away often work better. Tap any ⓘ to see what a term means.

2. Blades in the Dark details

Scoundrels are daring criminals in the haunted industrial city of Doskvol.

New to Blades in the Dark? Start here

When your scoundrel tries something risky, roll as many six-sided dice as their dots in that action and keep the highest. A 6 means it works. A 4 or 5 means it works, but at a cost. A 1 to 3 means things go wrong. With no dots, roll two dice and keep the lower one.

Scoundrels can take stress to push harder or dodge trouble. Too much stress leaves lasting trauma, and indulging their vice between jobs clears it. Tap any ⓘ to see what a term means.

2. Draw Steel details

Heroes are bold adventurers, built to be cinematic from their very first fight.

New to Draw Steel? Start here

When your hero tries something risky, you make a power roll: roll two ten-sided dice and add one of their characteristics. 11 or less is tier 1, 12 to 16 is tier 2, and 17 or more is tier 3. An ability's results are listed for each tier, and higher tiers are better.

In a fight, heroic abilities cost your class's heroic resource, which builds up as the fight goes on. Signature abilities are free to use. Tap any ⓘ to see what a term means.

2. Pathfinder details

Pathfinder Second Edition (Remaster), with the ancestries, heritages, backgrounds, and classes of Player Core 1 and 2. Open a list to read each option, or press 🎲 for a random pick.
